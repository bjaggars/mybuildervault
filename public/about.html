About MyBuilderVault

The builder platform that includes the client

Who we are

MyBuilderVault is built and operated by Jaggars Software Holdings LLC (JSH), a Florida limited liability company that develops software for small and mid-size businesses. Contact: support@mybuildervault.com.

What the product does

MyBuilderVault is an end-to-end project platform for residential builders — spec and custom — and, distinctively, for their clients. A builder organization manages estimates, contracts, change orders, allowances, selections, schedules, documents, photos, meetings, and decisions in one place; each homeowner sees a clear, builder-controlled view of their own project: what has been approved, what it costs, what needs a decision, and what happens next. It was born from the buyer's chair — the founder built its predecessor to manage his own custom home build — and that client-inclusive design runs through the whole product.

How access works

The application itself sits behind a sign-in wall because it holds builders' and homeowners' private project and financial records — that wall is a privacy feature. Access within the product is role-based and enforced at the database layer: builder staff see their organization's projects according to their roles, and clients see only the surfaces their builder has enabled for them.

Communications

The Service sends transactional email — sign-in links, invitations, project notifications, and support-ticket updates — from the mybuildervault.com domain. Recipients are account holders and people expressly invited by their builder. Support is reachable at support@mybuildervault.com.

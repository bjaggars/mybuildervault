Privacy Policy

MyBuilderVault — Jaggars Software Holdings LLC · Effective August 4, 2026

MyBuilderVault ("the Service") is operated by Jaggars Software Holdings LLC ("JSH", "we"), a Florida limited liability company. This policy describes what information the Service collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It is written to be read in full without an account, sign-in, or JavaScript.

Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, and optionally a phone number, provided when a builder organization creates your account or invites you. Project information: content that builder organizations and their clients enter into the Service in the course of managing residential construction projects — estimates, change orders, selections, documents, photographs, meeting notes, messages, and related records. Technical information: standard server logs, approximate region, and the identifiers required to keep you signed in.

How information is used

Information is used solely to operate the Service: authenticating users, storing and displaying project records to the people authorized to see them, sending transactional email such as sign-in links, invitations, and support-ticket updates, and diagnosing problems. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer project content for advertising.

Who can see your information

Access is governed by role-based permissions enforced at the database level. Members of a builder organization see that organization's records according to their role. Clients see only the project surfaces their builder has enabled for them. JSH support staff can access a tenant's data only during an active, reason-stamped, time-limited support session, and builder administrators can review a log of those support sessions.

Service providers

The Service runs on a small set of infrastructure processors acting on our instructions: Supabase (database and authentication), Netlify (hosting), Cloudinary (photo and document storage), Resend (transactional email delivery), and Anthropic (AI-assisted features that operate on content you submit for that purpose). Each receives only what its function requires.

Retention and deletion

Project records are retained for as long as the builder organization's account is active, since they document real construction projects. You may request deletion of your personal account information, and builder organizations may request deletion of their tenant data, by emailing support@mybuildervault.com. We will honor verified requests within 30 days except where records must be kept to meet legal obligations.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Data is stored with row-level security policies applied from the moment each table is created, and administrative credentials are held only in server-side configuration, never in the application delivered to your browser.

Children

The Service is a business tool for builders and their adult clients and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Questions: support@mybuildervault.com, or Jaggars Software Holdings LLC, Florida, USA.

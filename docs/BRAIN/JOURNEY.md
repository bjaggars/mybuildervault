# The Custom-Build Journey — Lead to Warranty (FILED 2026-08-14, Brice-ratified)

The full day-in-the-life map for a medium custom builder, lead through
warranty. This is the JOURNEY MAP of record; build order gets brainstormed
against it (open — see BOARD). Spec homes are a later, separate entry path
(build first, sell during) — parked by ruling.

Grounding (industry research, 8/14): the standard custom funnel is lead
gen/qualification → discovery → PAID design agreement → proposal/contract →
fulfillment/referral; custom sales cycles run 3–12 months BEFORE contract;
the professional pattern is paid pre-construction (Concept Design Agreement,
then Preliminary Building Agreement with everything selected, priced and
documented before ground breaks, fees crediting into the construction
contract); long-lead selections belong in the design phase.

## Stage A — Lead & discovery
| # | Scenario | Personas | The moment |
|---|----------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | The inquiry | Lead, Sales/Owner | Walk-in / referral / web form: "we want to build." Record born: wishes, style, timing, budget range, source. First response in minutes. |
| 2 | The questionnaire | Lead | Online intake: must-haves, lifestyle, budget honesty, land status, financing status. Filters tire-kickers before calendar time. |
| 3 | Discovery meeting | Lead, Sales/Owner | Virtual or in-office: vision, process education, portfolio + floor plans browsed together. Fit both ways; clear next step. |
| 4a | Land: they have it | Lead, PM/Super | Lot feasibility: survey, soils, utilities, setbacks, zoning/HOA, flood zone (Florida) — before promises. |
| 4b | Land: they need it | Lead, Realtor (Wendy) | Wish criteria → land search with realtor partner → candidates get the same feasibility screen pre-purchase. |
| 5 | Financing reality | Lead, Lender | Pre-qual: cash vs construction loan, the real budget. Lender intro if needed. |

## Stage B — Paid design (first revenue)
| 6 | Concept Design Agreement | Buyer, Sales/Owner | The FIRST PURCHASE: paid concept — preliminary plan, elevations, rendering. Commitment filter + revenue event. |
| 7 | Concept & the Design Center | Buyer, Bridgette, Eric | Walk candidate plans + variants, react, iterate. **The AI Design Center's natural home — in the sales funnel.** |
| 8 | Preliminary Building Agreement | Buyer, Estimator, Selections coord. | Paid pre-con package: near-final drawings, engineering, EVERY selection chosen and priced, site costs, fixed proposal. No ballparks. |
| 9 | Selections & allowances locked | Buyer, Selections coord., Bridgette | Design-phase selections (long-leads especially) priced against real SKUs — design center taps land here. |

## Stage C — Contract & pre-construction
| 10 | Proposal → contract | Buyer, Owner | Fixed-price contract presented in person; PBA fees credit in; deposit; deed/lot docs. |
| 11 | Construction loan closes | Buyer, Lender, Bookkeeper | Appraisal, closing, draw schedule established. |
| 12 | Permits & approvals | PM, Building authority | Plans to the building dept; authority dates tracked; nothing moves until issued. |
| 13 | Buyout & baseline schedule | PM, Estimator, Subs | Sub bids/POs committed against estimate; baseline schedule from template; long-leads ordered. |
| 14 | Pre-construction meeting | Buyer, PM, Super | Kickoff: plans walked once more, timeline confirmed, comms cadence + CO policy agreed. |

## Stage D — Construction
| 15 | Dispatch / crew / sub loops | Super, Crew, Subs | Loops 1–2 content: dispatch, checklists, magic links, drift. |
| 16 | The build's story | Homeowner, PM | Photos, published schedule, milestones — transparency without phone calls. |
| 17 | Change orders mid-build | Homeowner, PM, Estimator | The lanai T&G moment: priced, approved in portal, budget + schedule update. |
| 18 | Draws & inspections | Bookkeeper, Lender, PM | Milestone draws w/ lender inspections; municipal inspections gate phases. |
| 19 | In-build selection deadlines | Homeowner, Selections coord. | Remaining decisions surfaced at deadline (start − 5 workdays, computed). |

## Stage E — Close & warranty
| 20 | Pre-drywall walk | Homeowner, Super, PM | Walk the bones before cover: outlets, blocking, low-voltage. |
| 21 | Final walkthrough & punch | Homeowner, Super | Blue tape day; punch to zero. |
| 22 | Closing & handover | Homeowner, Owner, Lender | Keys, manuals, warranty docs, final draw, CO — and the referral ask at peak satisfaction. |
| 23 | Warranty life | Homeowner, Warranty/Office | 30-day list, 11-month list pre-expiry, 1-2-10 structure; claims ride the ticket engine. |

## The three through-threads (run across stages, not within one)
1. **Financing** — pre-qual (A) → loan close (C) → draws (D) → final draw (E).
2. **Paid-agreement gates** — rows 6 and 8 are the industry's tire-kicker
   filter AND the builder's first two revenue events, months before dirt.
3. **Referral flywheel** — row 22 feeds row 1; ask at peak satisfaction.

## Strategic reveals (from the journey ordering)
- **The AI Design Center is a sales-funnel asset (rows 7–9), not an
  endgame luxury** — it lives where deals are won. Reframes its priority.
- Nothing built is wasted: schedule/field/comms/tickets are stages D–E
  intact. The genuinely unbuilt territory is **stages A–C: the front door**.
- The 8/5 phase-taxonomy catch (Ocala 13-phase strip) maps cleanly:
  Financing/Land/Design/Selections/Permits ARE stages A–C.
